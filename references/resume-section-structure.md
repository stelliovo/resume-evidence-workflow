# Resume Section Structure

Read this reference before arranging final resume sections. Section names may adapt to the target job description (JD), market, language, and evidence emphasis, but the nature of each experience must remain immediately clear.

## Keep internships identifiable

- Put all selected internships in one clearly labeled internship section. Do not split internships across multiple broad sections unless the user requests a deliberate alternative.
- Do not mix internships with projects, freelance work, part-time work, campus organizations, volunteering, personal content operations, competitions, coursework, or informal collaborations inside a vague section such as `Relevant Experience` or `Work and Projects`.
- If the resume also contains full-time employment, use a separate full-time or professional-work section rather than relying on readers to infer status from dates.
- Preserve the exact relationship or role at entry level: intern, employee, part-time contributor, freelancer, project lead, volunteer, creator, or participant. A section title may not upgrade the underlying relationship.

## Choose sections from the actual evidence

Do not force every resume into exactly `Internship Experience` and `Other Experience`. Create only the sections the selected evidence needs. Plausible Chinese labels include:

- `工作经历`
- `实习经历`
- `项目经历`
- `兼职与自由职业经历`
- `校园经历`
- `志愿服务`
- `个人项目`
- `内容运营经历`

Equivalent labels in another language should preserve the same identity boundaries.

## Adapt names to the job description (JD)

Section titles may become more specific when that improves retrieval and remains truthful, for example `内容运营实习经历` or `市场与传播实习经历`. Use a general `实习经历` label when the internships span several functions or when a narrow label would distort them.

Relevant non-internship work may receive a job-description (JD)-specific section title, but it must remain separate from internships. Never use a tailored heading to imply employment, internship, scale, or ownership that the evidence does not support.

## Review before delivery

Confirm that a recruiter can identify within seconds:

1. which entries were internships;
2. which entries were full-time or formal employment;
3. which entries were projects, part-time work, campus work, volunteering, or independent activity;
4. why each selected section is relevant to the target job description (JD).
