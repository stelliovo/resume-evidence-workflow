# Resume Section Structure

Read this reference before arranging final resume sections. Section names may adapt to the target job description (JD), market, language, and evidence emphasis, but the nature of each experience must remain immediately clear.

## Preserve employment and experience types

- First identify whether each selected entry is formal employment, an internship, part-time or freelance work, a project, campus activity, volunteering, independent operation, or another relationship type.
- If formal employment exists, give it an appropriate `工作经历` or equivalent section. Do not place formal employees inside an internship section or let internships define the whole resume structure.
- When both formal employment and internships are selected, keep them in separate identifiable sections. Formal employment normally appears first for an experienced candidate unless relevance or the user's preference supports another order.
- When the candidate has only internships, use an internship section without creating an empty or misleading employment section.
- For an experienced candidate, retain an early internship only when it still adds useful evidence. Recommend omission when appropriate, but obtain approval before deleting it.
- Do not mix employment or internships with projects, freelance work, campus organizations, volunteering, personal content operations, competitions, coursework, or informal collaborations inside a vague combined section.
- Preserve the exact relationship or role at entry level: intern, employee, part-time contributor, freelancer, project lead, volunteer, creator, or participant. A section title may not upgrade the underlying relationship.

## Choose sections from the actual evidence

Do not force every resume into a student-oriented pair such as `实习经历` and `其他经历`. Create only the sections required by the candidate's actual evidence and career stage. Clear Chinese labels include:

- `工作经历`
- `实习经历`
- `项目经历`
- `兼职与自由职业经历`
- `校园经历`
- `志愿服务`
- `个人项目`

Equivalent labels in another language should preserve the same identity boundaries.

## Adapt names to the job description (JD)

Prioritize the relationship type in the section name. Add a job-description (JD)-specific function only when every entry in that section shares both the same relationship type and a coherent function, for example:

- `品牌与市场工作经历`
- `运营工作经历`
- `内容运营实习经历`
- `市场与传播实习经历`
- `市场策划项目经历`

Use the simpler `工作经历`, `实习经历`, or `项目经历` when functions vary, evidence is limited, or a narrower label would distort the entries.

Do not use vague headings such as `专业经历`, `相关经历`, `综合经历`, `互联网运营经历`, or `市场经历` when they hide whether entries are employment, internships, projects, or independent activity. Similar subject matter is not enough reason to merge different relationship types. Never use a tailored heading to imply employment, internship, scale, or ownership that the evidence does not support.

## Review before delivery

Confirm that a recruiter can identify within seconds:

1. which entries were formal employment, internships, or non-employment experience;
2. whether formal work experience receives appropriate visibility for the candidate's career stage;
3. whether section ordering fits both the candidate's stage and the target job description (JD);
4. whether any heading obscures or upgrades the underlying relationship type.
