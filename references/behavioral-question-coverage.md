# Behavioral-Signal Awareness

Use this reference only as an internal discovery lens for forms of problem solving that resumes often omit. It enriches the evidence bank; it does not add interview preparation to the skill and does not require one story for every category.

This taxonomy adapts common behavioral competencies and the installed BQ skill's story-mining model. Keep the taxonomy internal; ask users concrete, natural questions.

## Common question families

### Ownership and initiative

What interviewers may ask:

- a time the candidate took responsibility beyond an assigned task;
- something the candidate started or fixed without being told;
- a situation where the candidate kept an issue from being dropped.

Natural discovery cues:

- “有没有一件事本来没人明确负责，后来是你把它接下来的？”
- “有没有什么事情是你自己发现不对，主动去改的？”

### Cross-team collaboration and stakeholder difficulty

What interviewers may ask:

- difficulty working across teams;
- competing priorities or expectations;
- disagreement with a colleague, manager, client, or partner;
- repairing communication or reaching agreement.

Natural discovery cues:

- “这件事需要跟哪些人一起做？中间有没有谁的安排或者想法跟你不太一样？”
- “有没有出现过大家都觉得自己那部分更重要，最后需要重新商量先做什么？”

### Influence without authority

What interviewers may ask:

- persuading someone the candidate did not manage;
- changing a decision through evidence, trust, or explanation;
- gaining support for an unpopular proposal.

Natural discovery cues:

- “有没有哪次你不能直接决定，但还是需要让别人愿意配合你的办法？”
- “对方一开始同意吗？后来为什么改变了想法？”

### Ambiguity and problem solving

What interviewers may ask:

- moving forward without a clear process or complete information;
- identifying the real problem;
- deciding what to do first in an unfamiliar situation.

Natural discovery cues:

- “有没有一开始没人知道应该怎么做，只能先试一版的事情？”
- “当时你怎么判断真正卡住的地方是什么？”

### Failure, mistakes, feedback, and learning

What interviewers may ask:

- a failed project or missed expectation;
- a mistake and how it was handled;
- difficult feedback;
- what the candidate would do differently.

Natural discovery cues:

- “有没有哪件事最后没有达到原来想要的效果？”
- “有没有别人指出你的做法有问题，后来你改了？”
- “你们后来有没有复盘过这件事？当时有没有提到下一次要改哪些地方？”
- “后面再做类似事情时，真的改过哪些地方？”

### Prioritization, pressure, and limited resources

What interviewers may ask:

- multiple urgent tasks;
- a tight deadline;
- insufficient people, budget, or information;
- choosing what not to do.

Natural discovery cues:

- “有没有几件事情同时赶在一起，你必须先放下一部分？”
- “当时时间或者人手不够，你最后先保住了什么？”

### Results and persistence

What interviewers may ask:

- a difficult goal;
- recovering a stalled project;
- maintaining standards under pressure;
- persisting after an initial attempt failed.

Natural discovery cues:

- “有没有一件事中间差点做不下去，后来你还是把它推进了？”
- “第一次没效果以后，你们后来换了什么办法？”

### User or customer judgment

What interviewers may ask:

- understanding an unstated user need;
- balancing user needs with business or operational constraints;
- handling a complaint or difficult customer;
- changing an approach after user feedback.

Natural discovery cues:

- “用户实际在意的东西，和你们一开始以为的一样吗？”
- “有没有用户不满意，或者提出了你们没想到的问题？”

### Data and evidence-based decisions

What interviewers may ask:

- using data or research to make a decision;
- changing direction when evidence contradicted an assumption;
- choosing among imperfect signals.

Natural discovery cues:

- “你看到什么信息以后，决定不能再按原来的办法做？”
- “当时几个数据或者反馈不一致，你最后更相信哪个？为什么？”

### Improvement, innovation, and simplification

What interviewers may ask:

- improving an inefficient process;
- creating a new approach;
- simplifying something for users or colleagues;
- testing and iterating an idea.

Natural discovery cues:

- “有没有一件事原来特别麻烦，后来你们找到更省事的做法？”
- “你有没有试过一个以前没人这样做过的办法？”

### Leadership and helping others succeed

What interviewers may ask:

- leading without a management title;
- helping a teammate improve;
- setting direction or creating clarity;
- delegating, coaching, or stepping in temporarily.

Natural discovery cues:

- “有没有别人不知道怎么继续，需要你帮他把事情理清楚？”
- “你有没有带着别人一起完成过一件事，即使你不是他的领导？”

### Adaptability and change

What interviewers may ask:

- a sudden change in direction;
- adapting to a new team, market, tool, or constraint;
- abandoning work already invested in.

Natural discovery cues:

- “有没有做到一半，目标或者要求突然变了？”
- “原来准备好的东西不能用了以后，你怎么处理的？”

### Integrity, risk, and difficult judgment

What interviewers may ask:

- speaking up about a risk;
- choosing accuracy, safety, fairness, or user trust over convenience;
- handling sensitive or confidential information;
- making a decision with no perfect answer.

Natural discovery cues:

- “有没有哪次更快的办法其实不太稳妥，所以你没有选？”
- “有没有发现可能伤害用户、泄露信息或者引起投诉的问题？”

## Lightweight use

Do not ask every family for every experience.

1. Infer the two or three most plausible families from the experience's actual work.
2. Ask one broad event prompt after the core business facts are clear.
3. If an event appears, deepen only enough to preserve the user's responsibility, decision, process, outcome, and any retrospective or improvement that actually occurred.
4. Attach no more than one primary and two secondary internal signals when useful.
5. Do not maintain a completeness target and do not ask extra questions merely because a category is missing.

Absence of a story in one family is not a defect. Do not manufacture conflict, leadership, failure, or impact merely to complete the map.

Do not invoke a BQ skill, generate STAR/CAR/SOAR answers, predict interview questions, build company-specific interview framing, or create an interview handoff package. Those are outside this skill.
